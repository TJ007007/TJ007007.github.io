A FEW THINGS WORTH KNOWING

Before we build.

Can I use my own or second-hand parts?

Yes. Choose “I already have parts” in the wizard, then new, used or mixed. List what you have and any known faults. We’ll check suitability before accepting the job. Used parts are only sourced when you ask for them.

Is the wizard a final quote?

No. It shows how your budget and our fees fit together. We verify availability, compatibility, any licence cost and the work involved, then agree a fixed quote before ordering or starting.

Do you offer water cooling or BIOS updates?

No water cooling, including all-in-one liquid coolers, and no BIOS flashing service. We focus on air-cooled desktop builds. We can discuss workstation PCs, but not server racks or ASIC miners.

What does OS debloating mean here?

Removing unwanted optional apps and reducing unnecessary startup clutter with your approval. We don’t advertise disabling security, antivirus or Windows Update as a performance upgrade. Back up your files before any OS work.

What happens if something goes wrong?

Contact us so we can assess it. We do not offer an additional commercial warranty. Your rights under the New Zealand Consumer Guarantees Act still apply. Where our work fails those guarantees, we’ll provide the remedy required by law. We quote separately for unrelated damage or customer-caused faults, without limiting your statutory rights.

Can I talk to someone without Discord?

Yes. The on-site message panel lets you leave a question or build brief and read our replies here. It’s human messaging, not an AI chatbot. There is no word filter. We use basic security and spam controls, and replies depend on when we’re available.
